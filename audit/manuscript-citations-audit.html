Manuscript citation audit

Open findings from the source-to-claim check of journal.typ and supplement Tables S2–S3

NoteScope and method (17 November 2026)

Every citation-bearing sentence and table cell in journal.typ (Introduction through Conclusion, including Table 1) and in supplement Tables S2 and S3 was checked against the archived sources, following llm-wiki/AGENTS.md: catalog → evidence record → raw passage, and the archived PDF wherever numbers, tables, figures or garbled extraction mattered. Bibliography integrity (missing/unused keys, metadata vs. PDF) was checked for both files. Earlier audits were re-checked against the current text: journal Table 1 (claim-audits/table-1.md), Table S2 (claim-audits/table-2.md) and Table S3.

The three high-severity manuscript findings and the two high-severity Sanyal findings below were re-verified directly against the PDFs (Mikheev Fig. 6, Petek Table 3, Sanyal Table 1). Verdicts are conservative; where the archive is silent, the item is marked Not verifiable rather than filled in.

Most important issues:

  1. A4 (High), journal.typ:86–89. The Introduction says Allen’s 20% applies to “data with more than three features”. Allen’s “3D” graphics have exactly three variables (two independent and one dependent; Fig. 1A). The 20% is correct, but the population it describes is misstated.
  2. Fixed 25 Nov 2026, see Fixed citations. A36 (Moderate), journal.typ:266–268. Perceptual studies that “identify areas with high uncertainty … accuracy and response time” are attributed to Hullman et al. (2015). That HOPs study asked people to estimate properties of 1–3 distributions, with no spatial search. The description fits Sanyal et al. (2009), which is already in refs.bib. Update 24 Nov 2026: re-cited to sanyal2009user (now journal.typ:269–271); the “Both approaches” sentence that follows still needs rephrasing.
  3. Fixed 24 Nov 2026, see Fixed citations. A41 (Moderate), journal.typ:291–292. “In ERP waveforms, uncertainty is commonly shown using error bars or shaded confidence intervals”. The three cited sources give examples (Kappenman: ±1 SEM shading, not a CI; Pernet: bootstrap 95% CI; Allen: a recommended CI band, with the typical ERP panel described as having “no portrayal of uncertainty”). None of them shows how common this practice is. Mikheev’s 40% without error bars points the other way.
  4. A8 (Low), Table 1, Allen row. The 26 Oct audit’s single recommended wording correction (“Only 20% of audited 3D graphics portrayed uncertainty”) has still not been applied.
