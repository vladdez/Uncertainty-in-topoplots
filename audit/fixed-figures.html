V3. Hit/miss “most correct / most missed” frames highlight noise and ties

Severity: High · Fixed: 25 Nov 2026 · Figures: figures/task2_hits_misses_stimulus_by_vistype.png, task2_hits_misses_pattern_by_vistype.png · Principle: ch. 16 (no differences without uncertainty); ch. 4 (keep the accent for real emphasis)

Problem.

  • Row P3_16 (checked): every panel has 0% hits, yet Confidence intervals is framed “Most correct in row” and Basic topoplot “Most missed in row”.
  • Rows MMN_1 and N170_12: one “most missed” panel is framed out of several tied at 0%.
  • Cell sizes: cells hold n = 7–26, so these winners are mostly noise.
  • Pattern grid: it singles out differences such as 16% vs 12%.

Fix.

  • Preferred: remove the frames.
  • Otherwise:
    • name the metric (hit rate or mean score);
    • skip rows where the extreme value is tied;
    • print n or an interval.

The plotting code is in the UnfoldMakie worktree (uncertainty_project/working/task2_results/plot_task2_hits_misses.jl).

Resolution (25 Nov 2026). The frames are removed (the preferred fix): no panel is marked as most correct or most missed, and the legend’s “Panels” block is gone. Each panel’s subtitle keeps its hit rate and n. In the plotting script the frames are behind one switch (T2_SHOW_MARKS = false), so they can be restored. The Hit or miss page text now says why no panel is singled out.

    • add a reference line at 0 (ch. 23);

Resolution (25 Nov 2026). Both fixes were made, and the Feedback page shows them in tabs:

  • Theme × visualization matrix (first tab). Colour: median of the participant-level medians, binned in half-point steps (9 colours, brown negative to green positive; blue stays the Basic accent). One dot per participant, growing as a square from the lower-right corner; “● 1 participant” in the legend; empty cells white. Other tabs keep variants for comparison: grey cut-off for 1–2 participants, all cells with n, dots centred under the value.
  • Points with a median tick instead of boxplots, a dashed reference line at 0, light grey strips and a larger caption. Cells with 1–2 participants keep faded points and get no tick, as they get no median in the table.
