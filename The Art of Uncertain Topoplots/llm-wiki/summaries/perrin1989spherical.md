# Spherical Splines for Scalp Potential and Current Density Mapping

**Citation:** Perrin, F. and Pernier, J. and Bertrand, O. and Echallier, J. F. (1989)  
**Topics:** `neuroscience and EEG`, `statistical graphics`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/perrin1989spherical.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/perrin1989spherical.md)

## Why this paper matters

Derives spherical-spline methods for interpolating scalp potentials and approximating scalp current density. Relative to the authors' earlier thin-plate approach, it offers a simpler current-density derivation, faster computation, and better behavior in sparsely sampled scalp regions.

## Key findings

- Simulation tests led the authors to select spline order m = 4 for their interpolation method.
- The same framework supports smoothing noisy measurements and confidence intervals for approximated maps.

## Scope and cautions

- Short methods note based on idealized spherical projection and computing assumptions of its era.
