# Hypothetical Outcome Plots Help Untrained Observers Judge Trends in Ambiguous Data

**Citation:** Kale, Alex and Nguyen, Francis and Kay, Matthew and Hullman, Jessica (2019)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `sample-based visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kale2019hypothetical.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kale2019hypothetical.md)

## Why this paper matters

Two crowdsourced experiments compare HOPs, error bars, and line ensembles for deciding which noisy trend generated observed data, modeling accuracy and confidence across evidence levels.

## Key findings

- HOPs supported correct trend judgments at lower evidence levels than static alternatives in the tested tasks.
- Learning, presentation order, frame rate, attention, and confidence measurement qualify the result.

## Scope and cautions

- A single news-inspired binary trend task does not establish superiority for other uncertainty tasks or domains.
