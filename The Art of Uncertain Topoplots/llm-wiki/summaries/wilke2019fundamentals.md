# Fundamentals of Data Visualization

**Citation:** Wilke, Claus O. (2019)  
**Topics:** `visualization design`, `statistical graphics`, `uncertainty visualization`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/wilke2019fundamentals.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md)

## Why this paper matters

An example-driven handbook for matching chart form and visual encoding to the intended comparison, then refining color, uncertainty, annotation, composition, and publication output. The wiki provides a separate 29-chapter index and a catalog of source-linked design principles; use those for evidence-based review rather than treating this overview as guidance by itself.

## Key findings

- Recommendations are conditional on the data, comparison, audience, and medium; the book repeatedly presents exceptions and competing valid designs.
- The design chapters cover proportional ink, overplotting, color pitfalls, redundant coding, multipanel consistency, annotation, contextual guides, final-size typography, filled marks, and 3D.
- The uncertainty chapter distinguishes variability from estimate uncertainty and compares frequency framing, intervals, bands, sampled fits, and hypothetical outcome plots.

## Scope and cautions

- A broad instructional book, not a single empirical study; consult cited primary studies for evidence claims.
