# Visual Semiotics & Uncertainty Visualization: An Empirical Study

**Citation:** MacEachren, Alan M. and Roth, Robert E. and O'Brien, James and Li, Bonan and Swingley, Derek and Gahegan, Mark (2012)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `geospatial visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/maceachren2012semiotics.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/maceachren2012semiotics.md)

## Why this paper matters

Develops a semiotic framework for uncertainty symbols, then tests symbol intuitiveness with 72 geographically knowledgeable participants and aggregate-uncertainty judgments with 30 participants.

## Key findings

- Fuzziness, location, and value were among the most intuitive ordinal uncertainty variables.
- Intuitiveness and effectiveness were evaluated separately and did not reduce to a universal symbol ranking.

## Scope and cautions

- Purposeful GIS-aware samples, abstract maps, and selected symbol sets limit generalization to novices and continuous scientific fields.
