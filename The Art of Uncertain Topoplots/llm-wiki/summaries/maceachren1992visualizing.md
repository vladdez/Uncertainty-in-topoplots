# Visualizing Uncertain Information

**Citation:** MacEachren, Alan M. (1992)  
**Topics:** `geospatial visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `visualization methodology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/maceachren1992visualizing.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/maceachren1992visualizing.md)

## Why this paper matters

A foundational conceptual paper on uncertainty in GIS and cartographic visualization. It distinguishes data quality from uncertainty, considers Bertin's graphic variables, connects kinds of spatial uncertainty to map symbolization, and outlines interface strategies and evaluation needs.

## Key findings

- Exploratory GIS use requires uncertainty information because maps and computers otherwise invite unwarranted trust.
- The paper frames representation choice through data-quality concepts, graphic variables, uncertainty types, and interaction.

## Scope and cautions

- Conceptual and pre-modern-GIS; many proposed directions require later empirical validation.
