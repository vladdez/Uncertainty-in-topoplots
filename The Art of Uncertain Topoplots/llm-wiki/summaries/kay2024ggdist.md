# ggdist: Visualizations of Distributions and Uncertainty in the Grammar of Graphics

**Citation:** Kay, Matthew (2024)  
**Topics:** `software tools`, `uncertainty visualization`, `statistical graphics`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kay2024ggdist.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kay2024ggdist.md)

## Why this paper matters

Presents ggdist as an extension of ggplot2 that maps functions of probability distributions directly to visual aesthetics. The paper organizes a broad family of slabs, intervals, lineribbons, and dot displays and uses a distributional lens to connect Bayesian and frequentist uncertainty graphics.

## Key findings

- ggdist makes uncertainty designs beyond basic error bars, bands, and densities expressible within a grammar of graphics.
- Distribution objects and confidence distributions provide a common interface across Bayesian and frequentist workflows.

## Scope and cautions

- Primarily a design and software paper; it does not claim every supported geometry is perceptually validated.
