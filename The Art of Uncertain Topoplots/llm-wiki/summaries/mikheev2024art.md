# The Art of Brainwaves: A Survey on Event-Related Potential Visualization Practices

**Citation:** Mikheev, Vladimir and Skukies, Rene and Ehinger, Benedikt V. (2024)  
**Topics:** `neuroscience and EEG`, `scientific reporting`, `visualization design`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/mikheev2024art.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/mikheev2024art.md)

## Why this paper matters

Survey of 213 M/EEG practitioners about visualization tools, plot terminology, pain points, and preferences. It identifies inconsistent names, difficulty adding uncertainty, problematic colormap defaults, and produces recommendations for practitioners and tool developers.

## Key findings

- The community lacks consistent names for some common ERP plot types.
- Thirty-two percent of respondents reported difficulty adding uncertainty to ERP plots, and rainbow maps remained unusually common in EEG.

## Scope and cautions

- Convenience sample and generally small reported effects; the authors explicitly advise cautious interpretation.
