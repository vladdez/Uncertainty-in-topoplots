# Uncertainty Visualization for Biomolecular Structures: An Empirical Evaluation

**Citation:** Sterzik, Anna and Krone, Michael and Baum, Daniel and Cunningham, Douglas W. and Lawonn, Kai (2025)  
**Topics:** `biomolecular visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/sterzik2025.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/sterzik2025.md)

## Why this paper matters

Combines expert assessment with three crowdsourced studies (1,756 participants total) to evaluate nine metaphors for positional uncertainty across protein representations. It tests spontaneous association, directionality, and quantitative judgment instead of assuming familiar encodings are effective.

## Key findings

- No tested metaphor was strongly and consistently associated with uncertainty without instruction.
- Noise had clear directionality and relatively low absolute error, while the familiar sausage metaphor showed unintuitive directionality.

## Scope and cautions

- Studies used static 2D images; results may differ for interactive or stereoscopic 3D and for variants within each metaphor.
