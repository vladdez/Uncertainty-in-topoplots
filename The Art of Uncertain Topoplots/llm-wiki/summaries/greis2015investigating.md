# Investigating Representation Alternatives for Communicating Uncertainty to Non-experts

**Citation:** Greis, Miriam and Ohler, Thorsten and Henze, Niels and Schmidt, Albrecht (2015)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `non-expert communication`, `decision making`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/greis2015investigating.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/greis2015investigating.md)

## Why this paper matters

Compares twelve weather-uncertainty representations in a 90-person survey and four selected representations in a 12-person decision game, separating subjective ratings from actual choices.

## Key findings

- More uncertainty information did not automatically improve subjective judgments or decisions.
- Preference and decision performance did not align consistently.

## Scope and cautions

- The experiment had only 12 participants and used one simplified farming-game scenario; the PDF contains full conference proceedings.
