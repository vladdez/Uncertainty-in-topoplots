# Uncertainty Displays Using Quantile Dotplots or CDFs Improve Transit Decision-Making

**Citation:** Fernandes, Michael and Walls, Logan and Munson, Sean A. and Hullman, Jessica and Kay, Matthew (2018)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `decision making`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/fernandes2018uncertainty.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/fernandes2018uncertainty.md)

## Why this paper matters

A preregistered online experiment tests ten bus-arrival displays using incentivized decisions. Quantile dotplots and CDFs support decisions closer to the optimal policy and more consistent performance than common alternatives.

## Key findings

- The final analysis included 408 participants and used trial-level payoff relative to an optimal policy.
- Dotplots and CDFs reached about 95% of optimal performance on the last trial.

## Scope and cautions

- A laboratory-style online scenario and common utility function do not reproduce every real transit context or rider preference.
