# A Review of Uncertainty in Data Visualization

**Citation:** Brodlie, Ken and Allendes Osorio, Rodolfo and Lopes, Adriano (2012)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `visualization methodology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/brodlie2012review.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/brodlie2012review.md)

## Why this paper matters

A broad review organized around a visualization-pipeline reference model. It distinguishes visualizing uncertainty already attached to data from uncertainty introduced by filtering, mapping, interpolation, and rendering, then surveys methods for scalar, vector, and other data.

## Key findings

- The pipeline model separates uncertainty in input data from uncertainty introduced by visualization processing.
- The review argues that crisp displays can imply unjustified confidence and that uncertainty must be considered throughout the pipeline.

## Scope and cautions

- A 2012 snapshot of a fast-moving literature; it is a review rather than a comparative user study.
