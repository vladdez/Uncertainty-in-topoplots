# In Pursuit of Error: A Survey of Uncertainty Visualization Evaluation

**Citation:** Hullman, Jessica and Qiao, Xiaoli and Correll, Michael and Kale, Alex and Kay, Matthew (2019)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `evaluation methodology`, `literature review`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/hullman2019pursuit.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/hullman2019pursuit.md)

## Why this paper matters

A systematic survey of 86 uncertainty-visualization evaluation papers develops a six-level taxonomy connecting uncertainty inputs, displays, judgments, decisions, and outcomes.

## Key findings

- The review codes 372 evaluation paths across 86 publications.
- The literature emphasizes constrained inputs and confirmatory accuracy more than open-ended explanation and downstream decision quality.

## Scope and cautions

- The taxonomy characterizes published evaluations through 2018; frequency of evaluation choices is not evidence that those choices are optimal.
