# Practice Improves Performance of a 2D Uncertainty Integration Task Within and Across Visualizations

**Citation:** Kusumastuti, Sarah A. and Pollard, Kimberly A. and Oiknine, Ashley H. and Dalangin, Bianca and Raber, Tiffany R. and Files, Benjamin T. (2023)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `bivariate integration`, `learning`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kusumastuti2023practice.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md)

## Why this paper matters

Two online experiments test whether practice improves integration of two uncertainty sources and whether learning transfers across ensemble, summary, and distributional visualizations.

## Key findings

- Experiments analyzed 118 and 56 participants.
- Participants became faster and more accurate with practice, including transfer to unpracticed display types.

## Scope and cautions

- Online hardware and color were uncontrolled, and the integration task was abstract rather than domain-specific.
