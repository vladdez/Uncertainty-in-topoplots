# Bivariate Colour Maps for Visualizing Climate Data

**Citation:** Teuling, Adriaan J. and St\"ockli, Reto and Seneviratne, Sonia I. (2011)  
**Topics:** `bivariate color maps`, `geospatial visualization`, `climate and ecology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/teuling2011bivariate.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/teuling2011bivariate.md)

## Why this paper matters

Presents an easy-to-implement construction for sequential and diverging bivariate color legends and demonstrates it on global temperature–humidity patterns and European temperature–precipitation change. The aim is to expose spatial relationships that separate maps obscure.

## Key findings

- A two-dimensional legend can combine two climate variables without discarding either variable's mapped information.
- Bivariate maps condense related maps but are harder to interpret and require careful legend and palette construction.

## Scope and cautions

- Illustrative applications rather than perceptual validation; RGB interpolation and medium-gray center impose design constraints.
