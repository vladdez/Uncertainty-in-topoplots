# Empirical Studies in Information Visualization: Seven Scenarios

**Citation:** Lam, Heidi and Bertini, Enrico and Isenberg, Petra and Plaisant, Catherine and Carpendale, Sheelagh (2012)  
**Topics:** `visualization methodology`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/lam2012seven.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/lam2012seven.md)

## Why this paper matters

Derives seven visualization-evaluation scenarios from a review of more than 800 publications: work practices, visual analysis and reasoning, communication, collaboration, user performance, user experience, and algorithm performance. It tells researchers to choose goals and questions before methods.

## Key findings

- Evaluation scenarios distinguish what a study is trying to learn, not merely which method it uses.
- Of 850 coded papers in the reported venues, 361 included evaluations that informed the seven-scenario framework.

## Scope and cautions

- The venue sample reflects information-visualization publication practice up to 2011 rather than all applied fields.
