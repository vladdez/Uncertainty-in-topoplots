# Visual Encodings of Temporal Uncertainty: A Comparative User Study

**Citation:** Gschwandtner, Theresia and Bögl, Markus and Federico, Paolo and Miksch, Silvia (2016)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `temporal visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/gschwandtner2016visual.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md)

## Why this paper matters

A within-subject user study compares six encodings for uncertain interval starts and ends across interpretation, boundary, duration, probability, and preference tasks.

## Key findings

- Seventy-two valid submissions showed strongly task-dependent accuracy differences.
- Centered error bars produced ambiguous interpretations, while different encodings were favored for bounds versus probabilities.

## Scope and cautions

- Participants were visualization students, tasks used a single simplified interval, and the authors report task-design and cleaning complications.
