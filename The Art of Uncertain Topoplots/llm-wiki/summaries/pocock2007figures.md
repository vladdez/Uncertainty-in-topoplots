# Figures in Clinical Trial Reports: Current Practice & Scope for Improvement

**Citation:** Pocock, Stuart J. and Travison, Thomas G. and Wruck, Lisa M. (2007)  
**Topics:** `clinical visualization`, `scientific reporting`, `visualization design`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/pocock2007figures.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/pocock2007figures.md)

## Why this paper matters

Audits figures in 77 randomized-trial reports from five medical journals and develops practical guidance. Four types—flow diagrams, Kaplan–Meier plots, forest plots, and repeated measures—made up 92% of figures, but style and clarity varied widely.

## Key findings

- Trial reports averaged 2.3 figures and relied overwhelmingly on four recurring figure types.
- The authors identify substantial room to improve labeling, display of statistical uncertainty, and consistency.

## Scope and cautions

- The sample covers three months in 2006–2007 and five general medical journals.
