# Thematic Uncertainty Visualization Usability: Comparison of Basic Methods

**Citation:** Kubíček, Petr and Šašinka, Čeněk (2011)  
**Topics:** `geospatial visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kubicek2011thematic.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kubicek2011thematic.md)

## Why this paper matters

Compares basic methods for displaying uncertainty in thematic soil maps through user tasks involving value, uncertainty, and their combination. The results stress that relative performance changes with task and that learning or ordering effects can influence apparent intuitiveness.

## Key findings

- No single tested uncertainty method dominated all task types.
- Whitening performed well in parts of the study, but the authors note that earlier exposure may have contributed.
