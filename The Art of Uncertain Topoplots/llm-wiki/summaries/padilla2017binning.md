# Evaluating the Impact of Binning 2D Scalar Fields

**Citation:** Padilla, Lace and Quinan, P. Samuel and Meyer, Miriah and Creem-Regehr, Sarah H. (2017)  
**Topics:** `visualization design`, `empirical evaluation`, `geospatial visualization`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/padilla2017binning.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/padilla2017binning.md)

## Why this paper matters

Compares continuous and binned grayscale encodings for geospatial elevation fields across diverse tasks. The study shows a task-dependent speed–accuracy tradeoff: continuous encodings were often faster, while binned encodings matched or exceeded their accuracy.

## Key findings

- Continuous encodings often reduced completion time but never produced higher accuracy than binned encodings in the tested tasks.
- Binning is preferable when accuracy dominates; continuous encoding may help when speed dominates.

## Scope and cautions

- Uses grayscale elevation stimuli and specific tasks, so palette, domain, and working-memory demands may change results.
