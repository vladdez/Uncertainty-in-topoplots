# Data Visualization in the Neurosciences: Overcoming the Curse of Dimensionality

**Citation:** Allen, Elena A. and Erhardt, Erik B. and Calhoun, Vince D. (2012)  
**Topics:** `neuroscience and EEG`, `visualization design`, `scientific reporting`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/allen2012data.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/allen2012data.md)

## Why this paper matters

A critique of visualization practice in neuroscience, based partly on a survey of 288 articles, with a checklist for figures that reveal rather than conceal data. It is especially relevant to labeling, showing raw distributions, avoiding gratuitous 3D, and communicating uncertainty.

## Key findings

- In the surveyed neuroscience figures, labeling and uncertainty depiction were especially weak for 3D displays.
- The authors recommend informative displays that expose data structure and uncertainty instead of relying on standard decorative forms.

## Scope and cautions

- The journal sample is from 2010 and the article is a short NeuroView rather than a comprehensive systematic review.
