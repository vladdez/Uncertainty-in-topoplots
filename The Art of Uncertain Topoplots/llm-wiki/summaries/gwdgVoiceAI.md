# Voice AI

## Why this paper matters

A GWDG newsletter article describing the KISSKI Voice-AI transcription and live-captioning service. It reports use of Whisper large-v3 on local HPC infrastructure, privacy-oriented handling, and integration with BigBlueButton for lectures and meetings.

## Key findings

- Voice-AI uses Whisper large-v3 for transcription, translation, and caption generation.
- The service is positioned as a privacy-conscious alternative and can feed captions into BigBlueButton shared notes.

## Scope and cautions

- The PDF is a full newsletter issue; only pages 3 and 5–7 concern Voice-AI, and claims are service-provider descriptions rather than an evaluation.
