# ERP CORE: An Open Resource for Human Event-Related Potential Research

**Citation:** Kappenman, Emily S. and Farrens, Jaclyn L. and Zhang, Wendy and Stewart, Andrew X. and Luck, Steven J. (2021)  
**Topics:** `neuroscience and EEG`, `software tools`, `scientific reporting`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kappenman2021erp.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kappenman2021erp.md)

## Why this paper matters

Introduces ERP CORE, a standardized collection of paradigms, experiment scripts, processing pipelines, and data from 40 participants for seven common ERP components. It supplies reproducible reference data and analysis choices for developing and testing EEG methods.

## Key findings

- ERP CORE covers N170, MMN, N2pc, N400, P3, LRP, and ERN with optimized paradigms and predefined analysis parameters.
- The resource is intended for direct study use, data-quality benchmarking, and testing new analytic techniques.

## Scope and cautions

- The sample is 40 neurotypical young adults; direct reuse still requires checking fit to a new population and question.
