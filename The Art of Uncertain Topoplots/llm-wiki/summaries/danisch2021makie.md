# Makie.jl: Flexible High-Performance Data Visualization for Julia

**Citation:** Danisch, Simon and Krumbiegel, Julius (2021)  
**Topics:** `software tools`, `visualization design`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/danisch2021makie.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/danisch2021makie.md)

## Why this paper matters

Describes Makie.jl, a Julia plotting ecosystem combining GPU-backed interactive graphics, publication-quality vector output, reactive Observables, layouts, and extensibility through multiple dispatch. It is implementation context rather than evidence about a specific visualization encoding.

## Key findings

- Makie offers one interface for interactive, animated, raster, and vector visualization.
- Reactive Observables and extensible conversion and plotting pipelines support live updates and custom data types.

## Scope and cautions

- A software paper describing capabilities at publication time, not a usability or performance comparison.
