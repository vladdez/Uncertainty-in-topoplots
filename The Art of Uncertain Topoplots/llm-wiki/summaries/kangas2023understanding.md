# Understanding Uncertainty in Forest Resources Maps

**Citation:** Kangas, Annika and Myllym\"aki, Mari and Meht\"atalo, Lauri (2023)  
**Topics:** `geospatial visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `climate and ecology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kangas2023understanding.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kangas2023understanding.md)

## Why this paper matters

Reviews uncertainty in forest-resource maps and tests kriging, quantile random forests, and simulation on laser-scanning data with simulated responses. It separates pixel-level uncertainty from uncertainty in landscape-level characteristics and highlights the lack of usable joint prediction-and-uncertainty displays.

## Key findings

- Kriging captured pixel-level uncertainty in the test case, whereas quantile random forest slightly underestimated it.
- Simulation can estimate uncertainty for complex landscape-level characteristics that pixelwise intervals do not answer.

## Scope and cautions

- The method comparison uses a controlled simulated response and may not generalize to all forest-map models.
