# Confidence Intervals for fMRI Activation Maps

**Citation:** Engel, Stephen A. and Burton, Philip C. (2013)  
**Topics:** `medical visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `neuroscience and EEG`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/engel2013confidence.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/engel2013confidence.md)

## Why this paper matters

Documents a common misreading of thresholded fMRI activation maps and proposes color encodings based on confidence-interval bounds. The designs aim to show plausible effect-size ranges and support comparisons between conventionally active and inactive voxels.

## Key findings

- Conventional thresholded maps invite the false inference that colored voxels reliably differ from uncolored voxels.
- Confidence-interval-based maps improved respondents' ability to judge whether locations differed reliably.

## Scope and cautions

- The proposed encodings are initial designs demonstrated on limited datasets and survey tasks.
