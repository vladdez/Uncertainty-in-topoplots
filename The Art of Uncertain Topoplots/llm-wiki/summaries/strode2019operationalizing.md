# Operationalizing Trumbo's Principles of Bivariate Choropleth Map Design

**Citation:** Strode, Georgianna and Morgan, John Derek and Thornton, Benjamin and Mesev, Victor and Rau, Evan and Shortes, Sean and Johnson, Nathan (2020)  
**Topics:** `bivariate color maps`, `geospatial visualization`, `visualization design`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/strode2019operationalizing.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/strode2019operationalizing.md)

## Why this paper matters

Extends Trumbo's bivariate choropleth principles into three focal models for inverse relationships, a range of one variable within another, and direct relationships. Example obesity and inactivity maps demonstrate how palette design should follow the question a map is meant to answer.

## Key findings

- Different bivariate relationship questions call for different visual emphasis and color schemes.
- The focal models connect map purpose to palette design rather than treating bivariate colors as interchangeable.

## Scope and cautions

- Validation is through design analysis and examples, not a new controlled user experiment.
