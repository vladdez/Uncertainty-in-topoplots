# Uncertainty-aware Visualization in Medical Imaging: A Survey

**Citation:** Gillmann, Christina and Saur, Dorothee and Wischgoll, Thomas and Scheuermann, Gerik (2021)  
**Topics:** `medical visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/gillmann2021uncertainty.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/gillmann2021uncertainty.md)

## Why this paper matters

Surveys uncertainty-aware visualization across medical-image acquisition, transformation, visualization, and clinical applications. It evaluates approaches against requirements, considers how methods can form end-to-end pipelines, and identifies modality and workflow gaps.

## Key findings

- Uncertainty affects every stage of medical imaging and can influence diagnosis, planning, and treatment.
- Many approaches use separate uncertainty images, which can be hard to correlate with anatomy in clinical work.

## Scope and cautions

- Coverage and ratings reflect the literature available in 2021 and a small group of clinical collaborators.
