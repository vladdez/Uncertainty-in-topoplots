# Human Performance and Perception of Uncertainty Visualizations in Geospatial Applications: A Scoping Review

**Citation:** Tennant, Ryan and Randall, Tania (2025)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `geospatial visualization`, `literature review`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/tennant2025human.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/tennant2025human.md)

## Why this paper matters

A scoping review synthesizes 40 human-participant studies of geospatial uncertainty visualization, distinguishing performance, perception, preference, confidence, and decision outcomes.

## Key findings

- Direct comparison evidence is sparse and heterogeneous.
- Preference or reported understanding does not necessarily imply better decisions or task performance.

## Scope and cautions

- The review is geospatial, uses English peer-reviewed literature, and reports single-author screening as a limitation.
