# Uncertainty Visualization: Why Might It Fail?

**Citation:** Boukhelifa, Nadia and Duke, David John (2009)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `geospatial visualization`, `visualization methodology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/boukhelifa2009uncertainty.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/boukhelifa2009uncertainty.md)

## Why this paper matters

Explains the gap between enthusiasm for uncertainty visualization and its limited use in practice through an industrial underground-asset mapping case. The paper broadens the explanation beyond encoding technique to data availability, trust, standards, cognition, and organizational constraints.

## Key findings

- Uncertainty displays are limited when uncertainty metadata are incomplete, inconsistent, or themselves distrusted.
- Perceptual issues interact with social and organizational constraints, so technical encodings alone cannot ensure adoption.

## Scope and cautions

- A short work-in-progress paper grounded mainly in one industrial case.
