# A Survey and Task-Based Quality Assessment of Static 2D Colormaps

**Citation:** Bernard, J\"urgen and Steiger, Martin and Mittelst\"adt, Sebastian and Thum, Simon and Keim, Daniel A. and Kohlhammer, J\"orn (2015)  
**Topics:** `multivariate color maps`, `visualization design`, `computational evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/bernard2015colormaps.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md)

## Why this paper matters

Surveys static two-dimensional colormaps for multivariate data, defines seven quality measures, maps them to seven analysis tasks, and evaluates 22 reimplemented maps. Its task-based selection framework does not evaluate colormaps designed specifically for bivariate metric encoding.

## Key findings

- Elementary tasks impose conflicting colormap requirements, while results for synoptic tasks are more consistent.
- Ten evaluated colormaps passed all described tasks without exclusion on black or white backgrounds.

## Scope and cautions

- The assessment is measure-based and static; some published maps had to be approximately reimplemented. The authors explicitly exclude colormaps designed for bivariate metric mapping.
