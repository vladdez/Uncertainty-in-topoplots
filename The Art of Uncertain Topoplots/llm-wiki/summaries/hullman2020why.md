# Why Authors Don't Visualize Uncertainty

**Citation:** Hullman, Jessica (2020)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `visualization methodology`, `scientific reporting`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/hullman2020why.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/hullman2020why.md)

## Why this paper matters

Uses a survey of 90 visualization authors and interviews with 13 influential designers to characterize why uncertainty is often omitted. The study surfaces tensions between acknowledged value and practical reluctance, including audience concerns, workflow costs, lack of conventions, and uncertainty about consequences.

## Key findings

- Authors often value uncertainty communication while believing it may confuse audiences or weaken a message.
- The paper calls for usable text and visual guidance plus a clearer account of the implicit inferences viewers make when uncertainty is omitted.

## Scope and cautions

- Convenience survey and purposively selected interview samples do not estimate population prevalence.
