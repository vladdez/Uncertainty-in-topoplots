# Communicating Uncertainty to Policy Makers in Maps: A Systematic Scoping Review of Meaningful to Misleading Current Practice

**Citation:** Jain, Arushi and Walsh, Erin and Lal, Aparna (2026)  
**Topics:** `geospatial visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `scientific reporting`, `climate and ecology`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/jain2026communicating.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/jain2026communicating.md)

## Why this paper matters

A PRISMA-compliant scoping review of 66 studies using maps for climate-sensitive infectious disease in the Asia-Pacific. It evaluates whether uncertainty is connected to the visual evidence used for policy and finds that explicit visual communication remains rare.

## Key findings

- Only 3 of 66 included studies visualized uncertainty in their maps.
- Forty-three studies omitted uncertainty or mentioned it only in disconnected text, and 11 confined it to captions.

## Scope and cautions

- Restricted to peer-reviewed Asia-Pacific climate-sensitive infectious-disease studies and qualitative synthesis.
