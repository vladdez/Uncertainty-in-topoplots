# A User Study to Compare Four Uncertainty Visualization Methods for 1D and 2D Datasets

**Citation:** Sanyal, Jibonananda and Zhang, Song and Bhattacharya, Gargi and Amburn, Phil and Moorhead, Robert J. (2009)  
**Topics:** `uncertainty visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/sanyal2009user.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/sanyal2009user.md)

## Why this paper matters

Compares error bars, uncertainty-scaled glyphs, color-mapped glyphs, and uncertainty color-mapped onto a data surface using search and counting tasks on 1D and 2D data. The central result is interaction: effectiveness depends strongly on both task and dimensionality.

## Key findings

- Performance differences among the four methods depended on the assigned task and dataset dimensionality.
- Error bars were comparatively consistent, whereas surface coloring performed poorly for several tested tasks.
