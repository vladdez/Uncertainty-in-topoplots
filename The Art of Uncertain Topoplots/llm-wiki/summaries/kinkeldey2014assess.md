# How to Assess Visual Communication of Uncertainty? A Systematic Review of Geospatial Uncertainty Visualisation User Studies

**Citation:** Kinkeldey, Christoph and MacEachren, Alan M. and Schiewe, Jochen (2014)  
**Topics:** `geospatial visualization`, `uncertainty visualization`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/kinkeldey2014assess.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md)

## Why this paper matters

Systematically reviews empirical user studies of geospatial uncertainty visualization. It characterizes their designs and findings, emphasizes that success depends on the user's task, and recommends task-centered typologies for more comparable evaluation.

## Key findings

- Only a small part of uncertainty-visualization research had empirically evaluated proposed techniques.
- Task-centered framing is necessary because analytical, decision, and exploratory goals impose different requirements.

## Scope and cautions

- The evidence base is limited to studies available through 2014 and is heterogeneous in methods and outcomes.
