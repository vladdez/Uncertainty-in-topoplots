# LIMO EEG: A Toolbox for Hierarchical LInear MOdeling of ElectroEncephaloGraphic Data

**Citation:** Pernet, Cyril R. and Chauveau, Nicolas and Gaspar, Carl and Rousselet, Guillaume A. (2011)  
**Topics:** `neuroscience and EEG`, `software tools`, `statistical graphics`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/pernet2011limo.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/pernet2011limo.md)

## Why this paper matters

Introduces the EEGLAB-compatible LIMO EEG toolbox for hierarchical linear modeling over electrodes and time while retaining single-trial variability. It supports robust bootstrap inference and multiple-comparison correction in a massive-univariate framework.

## Key findings

- LIMO EEG analyzes all space-time measurements without requiring hand-picked electrodes or time windows.
- Its massive-univariate design is flexible and interpretable but does not directly model full spatial and temporal covariance.

## Scope and cautions

- Toolbox and software dependencies reflect 2011; the discussion notes covariance and computation limitations.
