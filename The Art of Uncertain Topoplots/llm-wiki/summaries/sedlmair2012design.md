# Design Study Methodology: Reflections from the Trenches and the Stacks

**Citation:** Sedlmair, Michael and Meyer, Miriah and Munzner, Tamara (2012)  
**Topics:** `visualization methodology`, `empirical evaluation`  
**Drill down:** [paper index](../index/sedlmair2012design.md) · [full extracted text](../raw/sedlmair2012design.md)

## Why this paper matters

Defines visualization design studies and derives practical methodology from 21 projects plus literature in HCI and social science. Its nine stages—learn, winnow, cast, discover, design, implement, deploy, reflect, and write—cover collaboration from problem selection through transferable lessons.

## Key findings

- A design study joins a real domain problem and users to abstraction, visualization design, validation, and reflection.
- Task clarity and where information resides help determine whether a problem is suitable and what contribution is possible.

## Scope and cautions

- Methodological guidance synthesized from author experience, not a controlled comparison of research processes.
