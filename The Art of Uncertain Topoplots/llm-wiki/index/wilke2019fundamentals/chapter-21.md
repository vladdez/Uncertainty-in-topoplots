## Actionable principles

<a id="principle-21-share-small-multiple-scales"></a>

### Keep small-multiple scales consistent

**Problem addressed:** Per-panel rescaling exaggerates small changes and hides differences in absolute magnitude.

**Recommended solution:** Use the same axis ranges and transformations across panels.

**Why it works:** Readers expect repeated panels to be directly comparable and commonly assume shared scaling.

**Applies when:** Faceted plots that repeat the same chart form.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** If differing scales are unavoidable, call them out explicitly in the caption.

**Book example:** Figures 21-3 and 21-4 compare degree trends with free versus common y-axis ranges.

**Search terms:** `small multiples`, `shared axis`, `free scales`, `facets`

**Evidence:** passages [p274-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p274-b002), [p276-b001](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p276-b001), [p276-b003](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p276-b003); figures [Figure 21-3](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p275-b002), [Figure 21-4](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p276-b002).

<a id="principle-21-order-panels-logically"></a>

### Order panels by a logical sequence

**Problem addressed:** Arbitrary facet order makes a multipanel display harder to scan.

**Recommended solution:** Arrange panels by time, natural rank, magnitude, or another task-relevant rule.

**Why it works:** A coherent sequence creates an expected reading path and exposes patterns across panels.

**Applies when:** Small multiples and repeated categories.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** The rule depends on the intended comparison; no single ordering fits every figure.

**Book example:** Wilke orders Titanic classes high-to-low, movie panels chronologically, and degree areas by average popularity.

**Search terms:** `panel order`, `facets`, `reading order`, `small multiples`

**Evidence:** passages [p277-b001](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p277-b001); figures [Figure 21-2](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p274-b001).

<a id="principle-21-consistent-visual-language"></a>

### Use one visual language across compound panels

**Problem addressed:** Changing colors, category order, fonts, or symbols between panels makes the same thing look different.

**Recommended solution:** Keep semantic mappings and ordering consistent throughout the compound figure.

**Why it works:** Consistency lets readers transfer interpretation between panels with little mental effort and may eliminate duplicate legends.

**Applies when:** Figures assembled from different plot types or tools.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** Different quantities may require different chart forms, but shared entities should retain shared appearance.

**Book example:** Figures 21-7 and 21-8 contrast inconsistent and consistent sex colors/order in an athlete figure.

**Search terms:** `compound figure`, `consistency`, `semantic color`, `category order`

**Evidence:** passages [p278-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p278-b002), [p279-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p279-b002), [p280-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p280-b002); figures [Figure 21-7](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p280-b001), [Figure 21-8](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p281-b001).

<a id="principle-21-subtle-labels-and-alignment"></a>

### Keep panel labels subordinate and panels aligned

**Problem addressed:** Oversized labels and slight misalignments make assembled figures look fragmented.

**Recommended solution:** Use unobtrusive, consistent panel labels and align axes and graphical elements precisely.

**Why it works:** Labels are navigation aids rather than data; alignment makes panels read as one designed object.

**Applies when:** Compound figures, especially panels prepared in separate software.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** Labels must remain legible at final reproduction size.

**Book example:** Figure 21-6 shows intrusive labels; Figure 21-9 exaggerates common alignment failures.

**Search terms:** `panel labels`, `alignment`, `compound figure`, `layout`

**Evidence:** passages [p278-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p278-b002), [p281-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p281-b002); figures [Figure 21-6](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p279-b001), [Figure 21-9](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p282-b001).
