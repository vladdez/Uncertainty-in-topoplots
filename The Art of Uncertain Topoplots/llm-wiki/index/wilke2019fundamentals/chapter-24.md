## Actionable principles

<a id="principle-24-dont-trust-font-defaults"></a>

### Do not trust default annotation sizes

**Problem addressed:** Software defaults commonly make axes, ticks, and legend text too small for comfortable reading.

**Recommended solution:** Increase annotation sizes and evaluate them in the complete figure.

**Why it works:** Legibility and visual balance depend on text being commensurate with marks and available space.

**Applies when:** Nearly all publication and presentation figures.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** Larger is not automatically better; oversized text can unbalance a figure.

**Book example:** Figures 24-1 to 24-3 progress from barely readable defaults to a balanced athlete scatterplot.

**Search terms:** `axis label`, `font size`, `software default`, `legibility`

**Evidence:** passages [p308-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p308-b002); figures [Figure 24-1](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p309-b001), [Figure 24-3](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p310-b001).

<a id="principle-24-review-at-final-size"></a>

### Judge text and mark balance at final reproduction size

**Problem addressed:** A figure that looks oversized on screen may become the only legible version after reduction.

**Recommended solution:** Scale the figure to its intended physical/display size and check text, points, and lines together.

**Why it works:** All elements shrink at reproduction; relative balance determines whether data marks disappear beside labels.

**Applies when:** Journal columns, slides, posters, and responsive outputs.

**Exceptions and qualifications:** If labels must be large, increase the data marks as well rather than changing text alone.

**Book example:** Figures 24-4 and 24-5 show that large text becomes acceptable after point sizes are balanced and the figure is reduced to two or three inches.

**Search terms:** `final size`, `reproduction`, `point size`, `text balance`

**Evidence:** passages [p310-b002](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p310-b002), [p312-b001](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p312-b001); figures [Figure 24-4](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p311-b001), [Figure 24-5](../../raw/wilke2019fundamentals.md#p311-b002).
