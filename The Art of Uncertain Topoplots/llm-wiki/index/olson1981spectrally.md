## Evidence-linked findings

- No two participants produced the same spontaneous color arrangement; 62% preferred the intended arrangement after seeing it. ([source passage](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p009-b002))
- Mean spatial-correlation error was nine percentage points higher for composite than separate maps, with above-guessing performance of 58% versus 86%. ([source passage](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p012-b005))
- Explanatory notes improved many attitude ratings and supported recommending composite and separate maps together. ([source passage](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p018-b002))
