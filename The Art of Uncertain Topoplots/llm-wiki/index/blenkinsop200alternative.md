## Evidence-linked findings

- With a greyscale legend, correct interpretation increased from 75.0% to 90.8% for experienced participants and from 25.0% to 52.1% for novices. ([source passage](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p006-b003))
- Random animation supported relative judgments at about 70%, but absolute estimates were below 50%; linked histograms improved some results. ([source passage](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p008-b003))
