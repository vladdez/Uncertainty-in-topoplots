## Research question

- How well do neuroscience figures expose data, dimensionality, and uncertainty, and what practices would make them more informative? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b006) (passage); [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b007) (passage)

## Contributions

- Combines an audit of recent neuroscience figures with an actionable checklist and a worked EEG example advocating richer, uncertainty-aware displays. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b007) (passage); [PDF p. 3](../raw/allen2012data.md#p003-b021) (passage); [PDF p. 5](../raw/allen2012data.md#p005-b012) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Descriptive audit plus design commentary and worked examples. |
| Sample | 288 articles published in 2010 in six neuroscience journals, containing 1,451 figures. |
| Conditions | Figures were coded by dimensionality and by whether labels, scales, and uncertainty were present or identified. |
| Measurements | Counts and percentages of figure characteristics; no controlled viewer experiment. |
| Analysis | Descriptive comparison of audited practices. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b007) (passage) |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-allen-audit-uncertainty"></a>

### allen-audit-uncertainty

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Only 20% of audited 3D graphics portrayed uncertainty; among 2D figures that showed uncertainty, nearly 30% did not identify its type.  
**Quantitative detail:** 288 articles; 1,451 figures; 20%; nearly 30%.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b007) (passage)
<a id="claim-allen-show-more"></a>

### allen-show-more

**Type:** `author_interpretation`  
**Claim:** The authors recommend revealing distributions and uncertainty and avoiding displays that hide structure, illustrated with an EEG flanker-data redesign.  
**Quantitative detail:** Worked example includes nonparametric 95% confidence intervals from 10,000 resamples.  
**Evidence:** [Figure 1, PDF p. 3](../raw/allen2012data.md#p003-b020) (figure); [PDF p. 3](../raw/allen2012data.md#p003-b021) (passage)


## Conclusions

- Neuroscience figures should be designed to reveal more of the underlying data and uncertainty rather than merely summarize it. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/allen2012data.md#p005-b012) (passage)

## Limitations

- The audit is a 2010 snapshot of six journals and the article is a short NeuroView, not a systematic review or perceptual experiment. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/allen2012data.md#p001-b007) (passage)
