## Research question

- What ERP visualization tools and plot types are used, what problems do practitioners encounter, and how familiar are they with uncertainty and colormap issues? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 3](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p003-b002) (passage)

## Contributions

- Provides a broad snapshot of ERP plotting practice, terminology, preferences, tool pain points, and visualization literacy, followed by recommendations for researchers and tool developers. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p001-b006) (passage); [PDF p. 16](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p016-b006) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Online LimeSurvey questionnaire open January–July 2023. |
| Sample | 213 completed convenience-sample responses from novice through senior M/EEG practitioners; recruitment used community mailing lists, social media, colloquia, and conferences. |
| Conditions | Four survey sections covering participant background, tools, eight plot types and names, feature preferences, controversies, uncertainty, and colormaps. Conditional questions produced smaller denominators. |
| Measurements | Recognition/use frequencies, preferences, reported problems, free-text plot names, and awareness questions. |
| Analysis | Descriptive percentages; FAMD reduced five proficiency indicators to two components, with the first used as proficiency; Spearman correlations/logistic regressions and bootstrapped confidence intervals examined associations. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 6](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p006-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 7](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p007-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 7](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p007-b004) (passage) |

**Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p010-b003) (passage)
<a id="claim-mikheev-omission-awareness"></a>

### mikheev-omission-awareness

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Many practitioners omitted or were unfamiliar with uncertainty and colormap guidance.  
**Quantitative detail:** 40% of 157 respondents who had published an ERP plot did not include error bars; 39% of 213 were unaware of colormap controversies; 68% had heard of 2D colormaps and, among those aware, more than 76% wanted to use them if easily available.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 12](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p012-b004) (passage); [Figure 11, PDF p. 13](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p013-b002) (figure)
<a id="claim-mikheev-topoplot-semantics"></a>

### mikheev-topoplot-semantics

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Respondents did not share a single interpretation of a topoplot series.  
**Quantitative detail:** 61% of 185 interpreted each map as a time-point snapshot; 39% as an interval average.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 11](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p011-b004) (passage)

## Conclusions

- ERP visualization practice is heterogeneous, and better conventions, defaults, and uncertainty support are needed in widely used tools. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 15](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p015-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 16](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p016-b006) (passage)

## Limitations

- The authors emphasize a likely biased convenience sample, conditional attrition, small effects, and uncorrected exploratory tests; prevalence estimates require caution. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 6](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p006-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 12](../raw/mikheev2024art.md#p012-b004) (passage)
