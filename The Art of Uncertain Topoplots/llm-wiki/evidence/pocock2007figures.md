## Research question

- How are figures used in randomized-trial reports, what recurring problems occur, and what guidance would improve them? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p001-b005) (passage)

## Contributions

- Audits figure type, style, content, clarity, and suitability, then develops general and chart-specific recommendations. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p001-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 17](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p017-b002) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Descriptive audit of published trial reports. |
| Sample | All 77 randomized controlled trial reports in five general medical journals from November 2006 through January 2007. |
| Conditions | Every figure classified and assessed for style, content, clarity, and suitability. |
| Measurements | Figure counts, types, and qualitative design characteristics. |
| Analysis | Descriptive frequencies plus expert critique and guidance development. |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-pocock-dominant-types"></a>

### pocock-dominant-types

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Four figure types dominated the audited trial literature.  
**Quantitative detail:** Mean 2.3 figures per article; flow diagrams, Kaplan–Meier plots, forest plots, and repeated-measures plots accounted for 92% of figures.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p001-b005) (passage)
<a id="claim-pocock-km-uncertainty"></a>

### pocock-km-uncertainty

**Type:** `author_interpretation`  
**Claim:** The authors warn that Kaplan–Meier plots without uncertainty can invite overinterpretation and show an example with 95% confidence intervals.  
**Quantitative detail:** 95% confidence intervals in the discussed example.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p005-b003) (passage)

### pocock-repeated-measures

## Conclusions

- Clinical figures are prominent communication devices but show inconsistent practice, including avoidable omission of statistical uncertainty. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 11](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p011-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p011-b006) (passage)

## Limitations

- The corpus covers only five general medical journals over three months in 2006–2007, and the recommendations are not a perceptual experiment. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/pocock2007figures.md#p001-b005) (passage)
