## Research question

## Contributions

- Audits uncertainty not only in text but separately in figure captions and map graphics, exposing disconnects between modeling uncertainty and policy-facing visual evidence. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p001-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p011-b002) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Systematic scoping review following a preregistered PRISMA-guided protocol. |
| Sample | 2,826 search results; 135 original-search studies underwent full-text screening; 28 additional candidates came from reviews; 66 papers were included. |
| Conditions | English-language, peer-reviewed primary research on climate-sensitive human diseases in the Asia-Pacific with at least one map; searches in PubMed and Web of Science in April 2025. |
| Measurements | Whether and where uncertainty was reported—in text, captions, or maps—plus qualitative assessment of mapping practice. |
| Analysis | Structured screening, data extraction, and qualitative synthesis rather than pooled effectiveness meta-analysis. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 3](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p003-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 5](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p005-b004) (passage) |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-jain-three-maps"></a>

### jain-three-maps

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Explicit uncertainty visualization in the included maps was rare.  
**Quantitative detail:** 3 of 66 studies visualized uncertainty; 43 either omitted it or mentioned it only in text; 11 reported it in captions without integrating it visually.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p010-b002) (passage)
<a id="claim-jain-policy-gap"></a>

### jain-policy-gap

**Type:** `author_interpretation`  
**Claim:** The authors interpret separation or omission of uncertainty as a risk to cautious, evidence-based use of maps for policy decisions.  
**Quantitative detail:** Not an effectiveness estimate.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p010-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p011-b003) (passage)


## Conclusions

- The reviewed literature contains a substantial gap between uncertainty in data or models and uncertainty visibly communicated in maps. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p010-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p011-b003) (passage)

## Limitations

- Only English-language peer-reviewed Asia-Pacific disease studies were included; the review evaluates current practice, not which visualization method is most effective. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 3](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p003-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/jain2026communicating.md#p011-b002) (passage)
