## Research question

- Can readers learn and use spectrally encoded two-variable maps for color ordering, spatial-correlation judgment, substantive information extraction, and favorable map evaluation? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 2](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p002-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 7](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p007-b002) (passage)

## Contributions

- Separates the evaluation of a two-variable spectral map into four problems: establishing a color order, judging spatial correlation, extracting substantive information, and assessing reader attitudes. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 2](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p002-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 17](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p017-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 18](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p018-b002) (passage)

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-olson-color-order"></a>

### olson-color-order

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Participants did not converge spontaneously on the intended two-dimensional spectral order, but a majority preferred it after it was presented.  
**Quantitative detail:** No two participants made the same spontaneous arrangement; 62% preferred the intended arrangement.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 7](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p007-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p009-b002) (passage)
<a id="claim-olson-correlation-hurdle"></a>

### olson-correlation-hurdle

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Composite maps produced greater spatial-correlation judgment error overall and fewer above-guessing responses than separate maps, although participants who successfully interpreted the composite map could perform competitively.  
**Quantitative detail:** Mean error 9 percentage points higher for composite maps (Z=6.10, p<.001); above guessing: 58% composite versus 86% separate; among successful composite readers, 58% did better with the composite, not significantly different from 50% at .05.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p010-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 12](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p012-b005) (passage)
<a id="claim-olson-information-attitude"></a>

### olson-information-attitude

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Readers extracted substantive information from composite maps, and explanatory notes improved many attitude ratings; separate maps were easier to read, while composite maps were often judged more interesting or authoritative.  
**Quantitative detail:** Many semantic-differential ratings improved by at least two scale points with explanatory notes.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 17](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p017-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 18](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p018-b002) (passage)
<a id="claim-olson-design-guidance"></a>

### olson-design-guidance

**Type:** `author_interpretation`  
**Claim:** The author recommends a prominent legend, presenting the two separate maps alongside the composite, and supplying explanatory notes to help readers cross the initial interpretation hurdle.  
**Quantitative detail:** No effect estimate; design recommendation synthesizing the four experiments.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 18](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p018-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 19](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p019-b002) (passage)


## Conclusions

- Spectrally encoded two-variable maps can support substantive interpretation, but readers benefit from explicit instructional support and access to the component maps. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 18](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p018-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 19](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p019-b002) (passage)

## Limitations

- Composite maps impose an initial color-order and interpretation hurdle, and the historical map designs, populations, and tasks limit transfer to contemporary interactive or uncertainty displays. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 9](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p009-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 12](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p012-b005) (passage)
- The stated overall sample of 466 conflicts with the detailed experiment counts, which sum to 465 (27 + 295 + 143). **Provenance:** `source_quality_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 7](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p007-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 12](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p012-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 13](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p013-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 13](../raw/olson1981spectrally.md#p013-b004) (passage)
