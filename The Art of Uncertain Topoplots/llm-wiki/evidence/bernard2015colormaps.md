## Research question

- How can static two-dimensional colormaps be selected according to the analytical task rather than treated as universally interchangeable? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p001-b003) (passage)

## Contributions

- Defines seven quality measures, maps them to seven multivariate-analysis tasks, and applies the resulting ranking procedure to 22 reimplemented 2D colormaps. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p001-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p009-b002) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Survey of designs followed by measure-based task quality assessment. |
| Sample | 22 static 2D colormaps. |
| Conditions | Seven measures/tasks and multiple backgrounds; maps below an 80% criterion were excluded in the task-based selection procedure. |
| Measurements | Computed quality measures and Borda-style ranks, rather than participant responses. |
| Analysis | Ranks were aggregated across measures relevant to each task and robustness was assessed across tasks/backgrounds. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 9](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p009-b002) (passage) |

**Evidence:** [PDF p. 13](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p013-b004) (passage)


## Conclusions

- No single aesthetic preference substitutes for task-based selection, although a subset of designs performed robustly across the tested criteria. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 13](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p013-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 14](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p014-b003) (passage)

## Limitations

- The assessment is measure-based rather than a controlled user study; several published designs were approximately reimplemented and conversion or clipping in sRGB can affect results. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 13](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p013-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 14](../raw/bernard2015colormaps.md#p014-b001) (passage)
