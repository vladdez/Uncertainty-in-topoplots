## Research question

- Which visual metaphors for biomolecular positional uncertainty are intuitive, directionally interpretable, and quantitatively readable across protein representations? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p001-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 7](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p007-b002) (passage)

## Contributions

- Evaluates nine uncertainty metaphors using spontaneous association, maximum-uncertainty directionality, and numerical estimation rather than assuming conventional metaphors are understood. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 7](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p007-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p009-b003) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Expert assessment followed by three online perceptual studies. |
| Sample | 1,756 participants across the three studies; the quantitative-value study included 423 participants. |
| Conditions | Nine metaphors across surface, stick, and tube protein representations; studies tested meaning, directionality, and inference of highlighted uncertainty values. |
| Measurements | Open-text associations, direction-choice accuracy, mean absolute error, response time, confusion, perceived accuracy, and aesthetics. |
| Analysis | Descriptive and inferential comparisons with means and 95% confidence intervals. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 7](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p007-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 8](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p008-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p009-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p011-b003) (passage) |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-sterzik-low-intended-use"></a>

### sterzik-low-intended-use

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** No metaphor was used in the intended way by a large majority across contexts, and the sausage metaphor's direction was especially poorly understood.  
**Quantitative detail:** Fewer than 70% used any metaphor as intended; sausage direction below 50% accuracy.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 9](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p009-b002) (passage); [Fig. 5, PDF p. 9](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p009-b003) (figure)
<a id="claim-sterzik-quantitative-performance"></a>

### sterzik-quantitative-performance

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Quantitative inference differed substantially by metaphor and representation; color performed much better than clouds and ensembles in the reported comparisons.  
**Quantitative detail:** Clouds MAE about 32 and 10 s; noise/contours/hulls MAE about 24 and 8 s; color more than 20 MAE units lower than clouds/ensembles, with representation-specific degradation for tubes.  
**Evidence:** [Fig. 9, PDF p. 11](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p011-b003) (figure)

## Conclusions

- Familiar-looking uncertainty metaphors cannot be assumed to be intuitive; performance is task- and representation-dependent. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 12](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p012-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 13](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p013-b002) (passage)

## Limitations

- Studies used static 2D images and within-molecule comparisons; interactive, stereoscopic, cross-molecule, and alternative metaphor variants may produce different results. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 13](../raw/sterzik2025.md#p013-b002) (passage)
