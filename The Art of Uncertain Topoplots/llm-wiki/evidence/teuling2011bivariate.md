## Conclusions

- Bivariate climate maps can reveal relationships compactly, but interpretive complexity is an explicit tradeoff. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 4](../raw/teuling2011bivariate.md#p004-b008) (passage)

## Limitations

- The applications are illustrative rather than perceptually validated; RGB interpolation and the chosen neutral center constrain generalization. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 4](../raw/teuling2011bivariate.md#p004-b008) (passage)
