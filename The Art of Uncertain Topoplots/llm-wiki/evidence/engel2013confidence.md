**Evidence topics:** `neuroimaging uncertainty`, `confidence intervals`, `thresholding errors`

## Research question

- Can confidence-interval-based color encodings reduce common misinterpretations of thresholded fMRI activation maps? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p001-b003) (passage)

## Contributions

- Proposes two encodings based on confidence-interval bounds so that both plausible effect size and uncertainty are available for active and inactive voxels. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p001-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 6](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p006-b002) (passage)

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-engel-standard-map-error"></a>

### engel-standard-map-error

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** A fundamental comparison error was very common with the conventional display and substantially less common with the second confidence-interval method.  
**Quantitative detail:** Error rate fell from 94% to 41%; p<.01 for Method 2, although 41% remained high.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p005-b004) (passage); [Figure 6, PDF p. 6](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p006-b002) (figure)
<a id="claim-engel-ci-map-purpose"></a>

### engel-ci-map-purpose

**Type:** `methodological_claim`  
**Claim:** Encoding confidence-interval bounds can reveal plausible effect-size ranges and discourage treating a non-significant voxel as evidence of no effect.  
**Quantitative detail:** Not applicable.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p001-b003) (passage)


## Conclusions

- Confidence-interval maps improved some interpretations but did not eliminate errors, so the proposed designs are initial evidence rather than a settled standard. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 6](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p006-b002) (passage)

## Limitations

- The encodings were demonstrated on limited datasets and small survey samples; substantial residual misunderstanding remained. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p005-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/engel2013confidence.md#p009-b002) (passage)
