## Research question

- Does practice improve integration of two uncertain quantities, and does learning transfer across display types? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p001-b003) (passage)

## Contributions

- Separates within-display learning from transfer among ensemble, summary, and distributional encodings. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p001-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 10](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p010-b003) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-kusumastuti-practice"></a>

### kusumastuti-practice

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Practice made participants faster and more accurate at integrating two sources of uncertainty.  
**Quantitative detail:** Experiment 1 N=118; Experiment 2 N=56.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p001-b003) (passage)
<a id="claim-kusumastuti-transfer"></a>

### kusumastuti-transfer

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Performance gains transferred to unpracticed uncertainty visualizations, indicating learning beyond memorization of one display form.  
**Quantitative detail:** Transfer tested across three display families; see Experiment 2 result figures for model estimates.  
**Evidence:** [Fig. 8, PDF p. 8](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p008-b002) (figure); [PDF p. 10](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p010-b003) (passage)


## Conclusions

- Evaluation of a novel uncertainty display should account for learning and cross-display transfer rather than relying only on first-exposure performance. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p010-b003) (passage)

## Limitations

- Online display hardware and color were uncontrolled, and the task was abstract rather than tied to a real scientific decision. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 11](../raw/kusumastuti2023practice.md#p011-b002) (passage)
