## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-kinkeldey-task-dependence"></a>

### kinkeldey-task-dependence

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Across reviewed studies, suitability of intrinsic versus extrinsic and other encodings varied with the task; adjacent maps could aid uncertainty retrieval while coincident maps did not always differ for data retrieval.  
**Quantitative detail:** One summarized study had 10 subjects; all 10 rated adjacent-map tasks easy versus 5 for the coincident map.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 10](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p010-b002) (passage)
<a id="claim-kinkeldey-preference-performance"></a>

### kinkeldey-preference-performance

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** User preference did not consistently track performance, and reviewed evidence did not establish that animation generally outperformed static displays.  
**Quantitative detail:** Narrative synthesis; no pooled effect size.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 11](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p011-b002) (passage)

### kinkeldey-task-typology

## Conclusions

- The evidence base is heterogeneous and task-dependent; more comparable, theory-oriented evaluations are needed beyond one-off product assessments. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 12](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p012-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 14](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p014-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 15](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p015-b002) (passage)

## Limitations

- The corpus covers work available through 2014 and heterogeneous methods prevent a simple ranking or pooled effect estimate. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 14](../raw/kinkeldey2014assess.md#p014-b003) (passage)
