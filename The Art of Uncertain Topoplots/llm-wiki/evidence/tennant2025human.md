## Conclusions

- The geospatial evidence base is fragmented and heterogeneous; future evaluations need clearer tasks, participant characterization, and comparable outcome definitions. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 15](../raw/tennant2025human.md#p015-b002) (passage)

## Limitations

- The review is restricted to geospatial, English, peer-reviewed work and notes single-author screening among its limitations. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 15](../raw/tennant2025human.md#p015-b002) (passage)
