## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Within-subject laboratory study. |
| Sample | 73 computer-science visualization students recruited; one empty submission excluded, leaving 72. |
| Conditions | Gradient, accumulated probability, violin, error bars, centered error bars, and ambiguation. |
| Measurements | Interpretation category, errors, completion time, probability estimates, and preference. |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-gschwandtner-interpretation"></a>

### gschwandtner-interpretation

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Centered error bars were interpreted ambiguously, whereas ordinary error bars were usually read as bounded uncertainty.  
**Quantitative detail:** Centered error bars: 47% bounded, 36% statistical, 17% neither; ordinary error bars: 89% bounded.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 6](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p006-b005) (passage); [PDF p. 7](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p007-b004) (passage)
<a id="claim-gschwandtner-task-dependent"></a>

### gschwandtner-task-dependent

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** No encoding dominated every task; encodings that supported duration and boundary readings differed from those intended for probabilities.  
**Quantitative detail:** Multiple task comparisons reached p<.001; exact results are in Tables 1–2 and Figure 6.  
**Evidence:** [Table 1, PDF p. 6](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p006-b002) (table); [Fig. 6, PDF p. 7](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p007-b003) (figure)


## Conclusions

- Temporal-uncertainty encodings should be chosen for the intended task and uncertainty semantics, not by a single overall ranking. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 9](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p009-b005) (passage)

## Limitations

- The sample had visualization training; the study used simplified single intervals and reports task-design, response-confusion, and cleaning problems. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 6](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p006-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 9](../raw/gschwandtner2016visual.md#p009-b004) (passage)
