### brodlie-two-uncertainties

**Type:** `theoretical_argument`  
**Claim:** A rigorous account must distinguish visualization of uncertainty in the data from uncertainty introduced by the visualization process itself.  
**Quantitative detail:** Not applicable.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 2](../raw/brodlie2012review.md#p002-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 25](../raw/brodlie2012review.md#p025-b005) (passage)


## Conclusions

- Uncertainty should be considered throughout the visualization pipeline rather than added only as a final graphical layer. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 25](../raw/brodlie2012review.md#p025-b005) (passage)
