## Conclusions

- The Bureau pursued faster color-map production and new map forms to improve public and policy understanding of Census data, while perceptual evaluation of cross-maps remained unfinished. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/meyer1975census.md#p005-b001) (passage); [PDF p. 17](../raw/meyer1975census.md#p017-b002) (passage)
