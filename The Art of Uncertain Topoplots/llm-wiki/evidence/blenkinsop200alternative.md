## Research question

- How effectively do greyscale, random animation, serial animation, and linked ancillary displays communicate uncertainty in fuzzy land-cover classifications, and does GIS experience affect performance? **Evidence:** [PDF p. 1](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p001-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 3](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p003-b004) (passage)

## Contributions

- Provides a task-structured comparison of four alternative uncertainty-visualization strategies across experienced and novice GIS users, combining quantitative accuracy with observation and questionnaire evidence. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 3](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p003-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 4](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p004-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 5](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p005-b008) (passage)

## Methods

| Component | Extracted detail |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Six-stage structured visualization test followed by questionnaires and observation. |
| Sample | 19 experienced GIS MSc graduates and 12 novice students; neither group had used the evaluated software. |
| Conditions | Greyscale display and legend, random animation, serial animation, and linked ancillary views for fuzzy remote-sensing classifications. |
| Measurements | Accuracy on relative and absolute uncertainty judgments, qualitative observations, and questionnaire responses. |
| Analysis | Descriptive percentages compared tasks and experience groups; testing was untimed and lasted about one hour per participant. |
| Evidence | [PDF p. 3](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p003-b004) (passage); [PDF p. 4](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p004-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 5](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p005-b008) (passage) |

## Claim-level evidence

<a id="claim-blenkinsop-greyscale-legend"></a>

### blenkinsop-greyscale-legend

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Adding a greyscale legend improved correct uncertainty interpretation for both groups, with a larger absolute gain among novices.  
**Quantitative detail:** Experienced: 75.0% to 90.8%; novices: 25.0% to 52.1%.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 6](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p006-b003) (passage)
<a id="claim-blenkinsop-animation-task"></a>

### blenkinsop-animation-task

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Random animation supported relative comparisons better than absolute probability estimates, and whole-scene judgments were substantially more accurate than judgments of isolated pixels.  
**Quantitative detail:** Relative comparisons about 70% correct (72-73% experienced; 68.3% novices); absolute estimates below 50%; most-likely cover 72% for a full scene versus 28% for isolated pixels.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 7](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p007-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 8](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p008-b003) (passage)
<a id="claim-blenkinsop-linked-support"></a>

### blenkinsop-linked-support

**Type:** `empirical_finding`  
**Claim:** Linked ancillary histograms improved some judgments beyond random animation alone, but the advantage varied by experience group and task.  
**Quantitative detail:** Experienced: 96.3% linked histograms versus 73.0% full random animation; novices: 74.2% linked versus 68.3% random.  
**Evidence:** [PDF p. 8](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p008-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p011-b003) (passage)


## Conclusions

- The authors rank greyscale as the most effective overall strategy, random animation second, and serial multiple views least effective, while stressing that effectiveness depends on task and expertise. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 11](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p011-b002) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p011-b003) (passage)

## Limitations

- The authors did not test animation speed or color and note that visualization effectiveness varies with task and user experience. **Provenance:** `authors`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 5](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p005-b008) (passage); [PDF p. 11](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p011-b003) (passage)
- The small purposive samples, untimed test, and single fuzzy remote-sensing setting limit generalization and causal precision. **Provenance:** `wiki_scope_note`. **Evidence:** [PDF p. 4](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p004-b003) (passage); [PDF p. 5](../raw/blenkinsop200alternative.md#p005-b008) (passage)
