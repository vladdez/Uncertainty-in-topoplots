## Explicit gaps

- No direct comparison exists for separate versus integrated uncertainty in EEG topoplots.
- Studies rarely separate costs of legend learning from costs of spatial correspondence and visual crowding.
- The optimal design may differ for lookup, spatial-pattern detection, comparison, and decision tasks; EEG-specific priorities remain to be defined.
- Interactive linking between a topoplot and a separate uncertainty view is suggested by prior work but not tested here.
