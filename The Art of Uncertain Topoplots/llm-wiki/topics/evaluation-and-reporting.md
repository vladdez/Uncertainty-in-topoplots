# How to evaluate and report an uncertainty-topoplot study

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/evaluation-and-reporting.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

What methodological evidence should guide evaluation and reporting of a new uncertainty-topoplot design?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

This matrix concerns study design and reporting, not the choice of a final encoding.

## Evidence map

- **`supports` — [Empirical Studies in Information Visualization: Seven Scenarios](../evidence/lam2012seven.md#claim-lam-goals-first):** Start from evaluation goal and research question before choosing method.
- **`supports` — [How to Assess Visual Communication of Uncertainty? A Systematic Review of Geospatial Uncertainty Visualisation User Studies](../evidence/kinkeldey2014assess.md#claim-kinkeldey-task-typology):** Use task-centered evaluation and report expertise and context.
- **`supports` — [Design Study Methodology: Reflections from the Trenches and the Stacks](../evidence/sedlmair2012design.md#claim-sedlmair-real-problem):** For applied work, ground abstraction and alternatives in a real domain problem.
- **`enables` — [ERP CORE: An Open Resource for Human Event-Related Potential Research](../evidence/kappenman2021erp.md#claim-kappenman-open-benchmark):** ERP CORE offers reproducible tasks, data, and pipelines for testing.
- **`enables` — [ERP CORE: An Open Resource for Human Event-Related Potential Research](../evidence/kappenman2021erp.md#claim-kappenman-noise-resource):** Benchmark noise distributions can support stimulus construction and quality checks.
- **`motivates` — [The Current State of Uncertainty Reporting in Ecosystem Studies: A Systematic Evaluation of Peer-Reviewed Literature](../evidence/yanai2021current.md#claim-yanai-reporting-rates):** Incomplete uncertainty reporting shows why source-specific reporting criteria matter.
- **`context` — [Figures in Clinical Trial Reports: Current Practice & Scope for Improvement](../evidence/pocock2007figures.md#claim-pocock-dominant-types):** Published figure conventions can be audited systematically rather than assumed.
- **`supports` — [In Pursuit of Error: A Survey of Uncertainty Visualization Evaluation](../evidence/hullman2019pursuit.md#claim-hullman-confirmation-bias):** Evaluation should extend beyond constrained accuracy to explanation and downstream decisions.
- **`context` — [Human Performance and Perception of Uncertainty Visualizations in Geospatial Applications: A Scoping Review](../evidence/tennant2025human.md#claim-tennant-sparse-comparisons):** Recent geospatial evidence remains heterogeneous and thin on direct comparisons.
- **`supports` — [Practice Improves Performance of a 2D Uncertainty Integration Task Within and Across Visualizations](../evidence/kusumastuti2023practice.md#claim-kusumastuti-practice):** Learning curves matter when evaluating a novel display.
- **`supports` — [Error Bars Considered Harmful: Exploring Alternate Encodings for Mean and Error](../evidence/correll2014error.md#claim-correll-within-bar):** Include diagnostic tasks sensitive to known encoding-induced biases.

## Research gaps

- A preregistered EEG-specific task taxonomy for uncertainty topoplots is absent.
- Future studies should report exclusions, denominators, stimuli, interpolation, reference, uncertainty definition, colormap, display size, and participant EEG/statistical expertise.
- Accuracy, response time, confidence, preference, calibration, and scientific decision quality should be analyzed as distinct outcomes.
- Open stimuli and code are needed to compare new encodings with conventional topoplots and separate uncertainty panels.
