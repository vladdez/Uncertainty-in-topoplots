# Why scientific visualizations omit uncertainty

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/uncertainty-omission.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

How common is uncertainty omission, and what explanations are supported?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

Audits estimate omission only within their sampled disciplines and dates; qualitative studies explain rationales but not their population prevalence.

## Evidence map

- **`supports` — [Data Visualization in the Neurosciences: Overcoming the Curse of Dimensionality](../evidence/allen2012data.md#claim-allen-audit-uncertainty):** Neuroscience figure audit documents omission and missing labels.
- **`supports` — [The Art of Brainwaves: A Survey on Event-Related Potential Visualization Practices](../evidence/mikheev2024art.md#claim-mikheev-omission-awareness):** ERP-practitioner survey documents omitted error bars and awareness gaps.
- **`supports` — [Communicating Uncertainty to Policy Makers in Maps: A Systematic Scoping Review of Meaningful to Misleading Current Practice](../evidence/jain2026communicating.md#claim-jain-three-maps):** Map review documents a large text/caption/map integration gap.
- **`supports` — [The Current State of Uncertainty Reporting in Ecosystem Studies: A Systematic Evaluation of Peer-Reviewed Literature](../evidence/yanai2021current.md#claim-yanai-reporting-rates):** Ecosystem audit shows incomplete reporting across multiple uncertainty sources.
- **`supports` — [Figures in Clinical Trial Reports: Current Practice & Scope for Improvement](../evidence/pocock2007figures.md#claim-pocock-km-uncertainty):** Clinical figure audit identifies omitted uncertainty as an interpretive risk.
- **`explains` — [Why Authors Don't Visualize Uncertainty](../evidence/hullman2020why.md#claim-hullman-omission-tension):** Qualitative evidence supplies audience, workflow, convention, and signal-obfuscation rationales.
- **`explains` — [Uncertainty Visualization: Why Might It Fail?](../evidence/boukhelifa2009uncertainty.md#claim-boukhelifa-adoption-barriers):** Industrial case expands causes to metadata and organizational infrastructure.

## Research gaps

- Audits use different definitions and denominators, so their percentages should not be pooled.
- Evidence is largely cross-sectional; causal tests of whether improved software defaults reduce omission are scarce.
- Omission rates specific to published EEG topoplots have not been directly audited in the archived collection.
- The consequences of omission for EEG interpretation and downstream decisions remain unquantified.
