# Sample-based and image-distribution approaches

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/sample-based-uncertainty.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

What does the literature establish about showing repeated possible outcomes rather than only intervals?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

HOPs and quantile dotplots have direct behavioral evidence in this collection; distribution-over-images is a general mathematical framework. None has been evaluated on EEG topoplots here.

## Evidence map

- **`supports` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Outperform Error Bars and Violin Plots for Inferences about Reliability of Variable Ordering](../evidence/hullman2015hypothetical.md#claim-hullman-hop-ordering):** Direct experimental evidence for improved multi-quantity ordering judgments.
- **`qualifies` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Outperform Error Bars and Violin Plots for Inferences about Reliability of Variable Ordering](../evidence/hullman2015hypothetical.md#claim-hullman-hop-time):** The animated condition took somewhat longer, though not significantly overall.
- **`explains` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Outperform Error Bars and Violin Plots for Inferences about Reliability of Variable Ordering](../evidence/hullman2015hypothetical.md#claim-hullman-frequency-account):** Authors connect performance to counting and integration of sampled outcomes.
- **`extends` — [A General Approach to Visualizing Uncertainty in Statistical Graphics](../evidence/petek2026general.md#claim-petek-distribution-images):** Generalizes sampled rendering to arbitrary statistical graphics.
- **`supports_theory` — [A General Approach to Visualizing Uncertainty in Statistical Graphics](../evidence/petek2026general.md#claim-petek-coverage):** Provides formal coverage arguments under stated assumptions.
- **`qualifies` — [How to Assess Visual Communication of Uncertainty? A Systematic Review of Geospatial Uncertainty Visualisation User Studies](../evidence/kinkeldey2014assess.md#claim-kinkeldey-preference-performance):** Review cautions that animation has not generally outperformed static uncertainty displays.
- **`supports` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Help Untrained Observers Judge Trends in Ambiguous Data](../evidence/kale2019hypothetical.md#claim-kale-hop-threshold):** HOPs lowered evidence thresholds in a noisy-trend discrimination task.
- **`qualifies` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Help Untrained Observers Judge Trends in Ambiguous Data](../evidence/kale2019hypothetical.md#claim-kale-learning-qualification):** Order and practice affected performance.
- **`supports` — [Uncertainty Displays Using Quantile Dotplots or CDFs Improve Transit Decision-Making](../evidence/fernandes2018uncertainty.md#claim-fernandes-decision-quality):** Quantile dotplots supported near-optimal transit decisions after repeated trials.

## Research gaps

- No study tests animated or aggregated sampled topoplots for spatial-pattern, peak, or condition-comparison tasks.
- Animation duration, sample count, temporal dependence, and accessibility need EEG-specific evaluation.
- Image aggregation can mix color and intensity in ways that may obscure signed EEG values.
- Coverage guarantees do not establish perceptual calibration or correct scientific interpretation.
