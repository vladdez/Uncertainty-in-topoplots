# Task and audience dependence of uncertainty encodings

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/task-and-audience-dependence.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

Is there a generally best uncertainty visualization, or does performance depend on task and audience?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

Across domains, evidence strongly favors conditional conclusions. This is a synthesis across heterogeneous settings, not a pooled meta-analysis.

## Evidence map

- **`supports` — [A User Study to Compare Four Uncertainty Visualization Methods for 1D and 2D Datasets](../evidence/sanyal2009user.md#claim-sanyal-task-interaction):** Technique performance changed with task and dimensionality.
- **`supports` — [Evaluating the Impact of Binning 2D Scalar Fields](../evidence/padilla2017binning.md#claim-padilla-speed-accuracy):** Continuous versus binned encoding showed a task-dependent speed–accuracy tradeoff.
- **`supports` — [Thematic Uncertainty Visualization Usability: Comparison of Basic Methods](../evidence/kubicek2011thematic.md#claim-kubicek-speed-accuracy):** Separate and combined maps traded speed against accuracy.
- **`supports` — [Uncertainty Visualization for Biomolecular Structures: An Empirical Evaluation](../evidence/sterzik2025.md#claim-sterzik-no-universal):** Authors explicitly reject a universal metaphor after three perceptual studies.
- **`supports` — [How to Assess Visual Communication of Uncertainty? A Systematic Review of Geospatial Uncertainty Visualisation User Studies](../evidence/kinkeldey2014assess.md#claim-kinkeldey-task-typology):** Systematic review recommends task-centered evaluation.
- **`supports` — [Hypothetical Outcome Plots Outperform Error Bars and Violin Plots for Inferences about Reliability of Variable Ordering](../evidence/hullman2015hypothetical.md#claim-hullman-hop-ordering):** HOP advantage was large for multi-quantity ordering but not universal for one-variable questions.
- **`context` — [Fundamentals of Data Visualization](../evidence/wilke2019fundamentals.md#claim-wilke-audience-uncertainty):** Handbook synthesis distinguishes compact expert encodings from more intuitive lay formats.
- **`supports` — [A Survey and Task-Based Quality Assessment of Static 2D Colormaps](../evidence/bernard2015colormaps.md#claim-bernard-task-mapping):** Computational assessment operationalizes task-specific palette selection.
- **`supports` — [Visual Encodings of Temporal Uncertainty: A Comparative User Study](../evidence/gschwandtner2016visual.md#claim-gschwandtner-task-dependent):** Six temporal encodings traded performance across boundary, duration, and probability tasks.
- **`supports` — [Investigating Representation Alternatives for Communicating Uncertainty to Non-experts](../evidence/greis2015investigating.md#claim-greis-preference-performance):** Non-expert preference and behavioral performance diverged.
- **`qualifies` — [Uncertainty in Geographic Data on Bivariate Maps: An Examination of Visualization Preference and Decision Making](../evidence/scholz2014uncertainty.md#claim-scholz-preference):** Preference rankings alone do not establish decision effectiveness.
- **`supports` — [Human Performance and Perception of Uncertainty Visualizations in Geospatial Applications: A Scoping Review](../evidence/tennant2025human.md#claim-tennant-preference-divergence):** Recent scoping review warns against treating preference as performance.

## Research gaps

- EEG-domain tasks need explicit definition: amplitude lookup, sign detection, spatial extent, peak localization, condition comparison, and inference are not interchangeable.
- Expertise and statistical literacy are inconsistently measured across existing studies.
- Few studies test accessibility, color-vision diversity, or small-multiple publication constraints together with uncertainty comprehension.
- Performance, preference, confidence, and scientific validity can diverge and should not be collapsed into one outcome.
