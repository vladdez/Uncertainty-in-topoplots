# Integrated versus separate value–uncertainty displays

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/integrated-versus-separate.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

When should value and uncertainty be integrated in one display rather than separated?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

The evidence is mixed because studies use different tasks, maps, encodings, and audiences. Treat apparent disagreement as task dependence, not as a simple contradiction.

## Evidence map

- **`supports_integration` — [Value-Suppressing Uncertainty Palettes](../evidence/correll2018task.md#claim-correll-superposition-accuracy):** Superposition outperformed juxtaposition for a targeted value-plus-uncertainty identification task.
- **`supports_integration` — [Value-Suppressing Uncertainty Palettes](../evidence/correll2018task.md#claim-correll-integration-interpretation):** Authors recommend integration or interaction when information fusion is required.
- **`mixed` — [Thematic Uncertainty Visualization Usability: Comparison of Basic Methods](../evidence/kubicek2011thematic.md#claim-kubicek-speed-accuracy):** Separate maps were more accurate but slower than a combined whitening map.
- **`mixed` — [How to Assess Visual Communication of Uncertainty? A Systematic Review of Geospatial Uncertainty Visualisation User Studies](../evidence/kinkeldey2014assess.md#claim-kinkeldey-task-dependence):** Review finds adjacent maps may aid uncertainty retrieval while other outcomes differ by task.
- **`supports_integration` — [Uncertainty-aware Visualization in Medical Imaging: A Survey](../evidence/gillmann2021uncertainty.md#claim-gillmann-separated-images):** Medical-imaging survey flags correspondence costs for separate uncertainty images.
- **`supports_integration` — [Bivariate Colour Maps for Visualizing Climate Data](../evidence/teuling2011bivariate.md#claim-teuling-joint-pattern):** Illustrative argument that bivariate maps reveal joint spatial patterns.
- **`qualifies` — [Bivariate Colour Maps for Visualizing Climate Data](../evidence/teuling2011bivariate.md#claim-teuling-difficulty):** The same paper acknowledges increased interpretation difficulty.
- **`challenges_integration` — [An Empirical Inquiry Concerning Human Understanding of Two-Variable Color Maps](../evidence/wainer1980colormaps.md#claim-wainer-bivariate-cost):** Early experiments show large elementary-reading and legend-learning costs for bivariate maps.
- **`qualifies` — [Visual Semiotics & Uncertainty Visualization: An Empirical Study](../evidence/maceachren2012semiotics.md#claim-maceachren-aggregate):** Multi-symbol effectiveness varied by uncertainty condition and symbol family.
- **`qualifies` — [Practice Improves Performance of a 2D Uncertainty Integration Task Within and Across Visualizations](../evidence/kusumastuti2023practice.md#claim-kusumastuti-transfer):** Integration performance changes with practice and transfers across display families.

## Research gaps

- No direct comparison exists for separate versus integrated uncertainty in EEG topoplots.
- Studies rarely separate costs of legend learning from costs of spatial correspondence and visual crowding.
- The optimal design may differ for lookup, spatial-pattern detection, comparison, and decision tasks; EEG-specific priorities remain to be defined.
- Interactive linking between a topoplot and a separate uncertainty view is suggested by prior work but not tested here.
