# Bivariate colour-map origins, readability, and later quality work

[Evidence matrix](../matrices/bivariate-map-history-and-evaluation.md) · [Claim catalog](../catalog/claims.md)

## Research question

What does the reviewed literature establish about the origins, readability, and later assessment of bivariate colour maps?

## Scope and synthesis boundary

The sources cover different map classes and methods. Meyer documents Census development, Wainer tests the resulting two-variable maps, Strode addresses bivariate choropleth design, and Bernard assesses multivariate 2D colormaps while explicitly excluding bivariate metric maps. These records form a historical sequence, not one directly comparable experiment series.

## Evidence map

- **`historical_documentation` — [Color Statistical Mapping by the U.S. Bureau of the Census](../evidence/meyer1975census.md#claim-meyer-cross-map-origin):** Primary documentation dates the Bureau's cross-map proposal to 1974 and its publication to 1975.
- **`design_rationale` — [Color Statistical Mapping by the U.S. Bureau of the Census](../evidence/meyer1975census.md#claim-meyer-cross-map-purpose):** The intended relational-reading benefit is an author interpretation, not a measured outcome.
- **`qualifies` — [Color Statistical Mapping by the U.S. Bureau of the Census](../evidence/meyer1975census.md#claim-meyer-evaluation-status):** The source states that reader evaluation was still underway.
- **`challenges` — [An Empirical Inquiry Concerning Human Understanding of Two-Variable Color Maps](../evidence/wainer1980colormaps.md#claim-wainer-bivariate-cost):** Controlled elementary-reading trials found a threefold mean error rate despite slightly faster bivariate responses.
- **`challenges` — [An Empirical Inquiry Concerning Human Understanding of Two-Variable Color Maps](../evidence/wainer1980colormaps.md#claim-wainer-legend-cost):** Removing the legend produced severe bivariate performance degradation.
- **`later_design_work` — [Operationalizing Trumbo's Principles of Bivariate Choropleth Map Design](../evidence/strode2019operationalizing.md#claim-strode-question-first):** Later work structures bivariate choropleth palettes around the relationship and reader question.
- **`adjacent_quality_method` — [A Survey and Task-Based Quality Assessment of Static 2D Colormaps](../evidence/bernard2015colormaps.md#claim-bernard-task-mapping):** Task-based quality assessment is relevant by analogy but was not applied to bivariate metric maps.

## Research gaps

- Meyer reports design rationale and ongoing research rather than reader-performance results.
- Wainer's small student sample, historic palettes, and elementary lookup tasks do not establish performance for modern bivariate uncertainty maps.
- Bernard's task-based assessment cannot be cited as a direct evaluation of bivariate metric maps because that class was explicitly excluded.
